4. Unfair-feeling deaths at slice edges → our forgiving collision ("edge grace") and a solver-verified fairness budget per level.
5. Same-looking levels → 12 worlds × pattern library × new mechanics introduced over time.

5. **Market trends to follow (2025–26):**
   - Hybrid-casual meta (missions, passes, events) keeps players better than pure hyper-casual.
   - Unlock features gradually: Endless at level 8, Daily Challenge at 13, the wheel at 5.
   - Teach the game by doing, not with text.
   - No ads until the player is hooked.

## 4. Core gameplay (genre base + our twists)

**Base loop** — ball bounces in place; player drags left/right to spin the tower; ball falls through gaps; each ring passed shatters and scores; landing on a hazard ends the run; reach the finish pad to clear the level.

**Our feel & fairness**
- Fixed-timestep physics (120 Hz) + interpolation → identical feel at 60/90 Hz.
- **Edge grace**: ball dies only when its centre is ≥ 4° inside a hazard; near-edge landings count as safe.
- Sensitivity slider; flick inertia with soft damping (tower keeps a little momentum, feels premium).
- Squash & stretch, impact splats, ring-shatter shards with real spin, camera spring follow.

**Combo → BLAZE mode (our name)**
- Fall through 3+ rings without bouncing → ball ignites (fire trail, glow, heartbeat bass); the next contact **smashes** that ring, even a hazard. Combo counter scales score (×2, ×3…).
- Rising musical pitch on each ring passed in a combo (very satisfying, unique audio signature).

**New mechanics introduced over time** (each gets a one-time tooltip banner the first time it appears)

| From level | Mechanic | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic rings, coins in gaps | wide gaps |
| 3 | Static hazards | glowing + striped (colour-blind safe) |
| 7 | Blaze chute | 3 aligned gaps teach Blaze |
| 10 | **Guardian Gate** (boss) | every 10th level; each bounce cracks it, Blaze smashes it instantly → always beatable |
| 12 | Power-ups | Shield bubble, Coin Magnet, Slow-Mo, Drill (auto-smash 5 rings) |
| 15 | Crumble tiles | crack on 1st bounce, break into a gap on 2nd |
| 21 | Counter-spinning rings | ring rotates on its own |
| 31 | Sliding hazards | hazard arc glides back and forth |
| 41 | Pulse hazards | deadly only while lit; 0.4 s warning flicker |
| 51 | Shutter gates | gap opens/closes on a rhythm |
| 61 | Boost pads | positive tile: drops you through 2 rings instantly |
| 81 | Mirror rings | ring spins opposite to your drag |
| 1000+ | Mastery tiers | each 1000 levels: faster motions, denser mixes (still solver-capped) |

**Level end — "Coin Vault" finale**: the last rings are vault rings; the bigger your combo entering them, the more you smash and the more coins burst out. Then the finish pad → celebration.

**Stars per level (★★★)**: ★ clear · ★★ clear without revive · ★★★ clear without revive **and** trigger Blaze at least once. Stars feed the Trophy Road.

## 5. The 10,000-level script (difficulty from easy → hard)

All levels come from one deterministic generator: `LevelGenerator.generate(level)` with a seeded RNG (same level = same layout on every device, nothing to download, 0 MB of level files).

### 5.1 Difficulty index D(level)
- Levels **1–10** (Warm-up band): D = 0.02 → 0.10, hand-tuned templates. **Guaranteed win**: no hazard ever sits under a gap, wide gaps, low rotation needed. The player can only die by actively spinning onto a hazard, and levels 1–2 have no hazards at all.
- Levels **11–10,000**: `D = 0.12 + 0.70·(1 − e^(−(L−10)/380))^0.8 + 0.18·log10(L/10)/3`
  — **strictly increasing every level** (verified for all 10,000), fast learning curve early, slow mastery climb late.

### 5.2 What D controls (sample values)

| Level | D | Rings | Gap width | Hazard share | Max gap shift | Trap landings | Moving parts | Speed | Solver cap (°/s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.02 | 11 | 109° | 0% | 48° | 0% | 0% | 1.00× | 155 |
| 5 | 0.06 | 15 | 106° | 10% | 53° | 0% | 0% | 1.01× | 165 |
| 10 | 0.10 | 20 | 103° | 12% | 60° | 0% | 0% | 1.02× | 177 |
| 11 | 0.13 | 21 | 100° | 14% | 64° | 0% | 0% | 1.03× | 185 |
| 30 | 0.21 | 23 | 94° | 17% | 76° | 1% | 5% | 1.05× | 208 |
| 100 | 0.38 | 29 | 82° | 25% | 100° | 12% | 17% | 1.08× | 253 |
| 300 | 0.63 | 36 | 63° | 36% | 137° | 30% | 36% | 1.14× | 321 |
| 1000 | 0.90 | 43 | 44° | 48% | 175° | 48% | 56% | 1.20× | 393 |
| 5000 | 0.98 | 46 | 37° | 51% | 187° | 54% | 60% | 1.22× | 415 |
| 10000 | 1.00 | 46 | 36° | 52% | 190° | 55% | 60% | 1.22× | 420 |

"Trap landing" = a hazard directly under the previous gap, so you must spin while falling. "Solver cap" = the fastest spin a level is ever allowed to require.

### 5.3 Pattern library (levels feel designed, not random)
Staircase · Zig-zag · Spiral chute · Twin gaps · Hazard sandwich · Checker crown · Pinwheel · Vault drop · Breather run (2–3 easy rings after a hard section). The generator stitches 4–8-ring segments from these, weighted by D and the world.

### 5.4 "Every level must be perfect" — the fairness solver
For each generated level a solver simulates a skilled-but-human player:
- Max spin speed = solver cap above; reaction delay 0.18–0.35 s; ball/edge margins included.
- Dynamic programming over (ring, angle, time) including moving/pulsing parts.
- Must find a path from top to finish; every ring must have a safe landing zone ≥ ball width + margin; boss gate must be breakable without Blaze.
- If a ring fails, it is regenerated with a sub-seed; if still failing, replaced by a safe pattern.

**Unit test (runs on the computer, no device)**: generates and solves **all 10,000 levels** and asserts: solvable, D strictly increasing, levels 1–10 have zero trap landings and no hazard under any gap, ring count within bounds, no two consecutive levels identical. Also prints a difficulty report.

### 5.5 Chapters & bosses
- 10 levels = 1 chapter = 1 world visit; level 10, 20, 30 … is a **Guardian Gate** level (boss + guaranteed chest).
- 12 worlds cycle every 120 levels; each new cycle is a "remix" with a new palette (Dawn, Dusk, Night, Neon …) so level 10,000 still looks fresh.

## 7. Graphics plan (the playground gets the most time)

**Engine**: custom OpenGL ES 3.0 renderer on a `GLSurfaceView` (Kotlin), Compose HUD on top. 4× MSAA (cheap on Mali), sRGB-correct shading.

**Render passes**
1. Shadow map (directional key light) → soft PCF shadows of ball and rings onto rings below.
2. Sky: animated gradient + sun/moon glow; 3 parallax backdrop layers generated from noise per world; depth fog.
3. Tower pillar: cylinder with per-world procedural material (stripes, grain, ice, metal).
4. Rings: **seamless beveled arcs** generated per ring (top, rounded bevel, sides, underside), instanced; per-world shader patterns; hazards glow and pulse; fake AO near the pillar.
5. Splat decals: procedurally generated splat textures, projected on ring tops, rotate with ring, fade over time.
6. Shards: broken arcs split into 3–6 chunk meshes with spin, gravity, fade.
7. Ball: icosphere with skin shader (gloss, metal, emissive, patterns), squash & stretch, ribbon trail, Blaze aura.
8. Particles: GPU-instanced billboards (droplets, sparks, confetti, snow, embers, bubbles, petals) ~1,500 max.
9. Post FX: bloom (half/quarter res), vignette, per-world colour grade, impact flash + light chromatic pulse on smashes, camera shake.

**Camera moments**: level-start swoop down the tower (1.2 s preview), spring follow, punch-zoom on Blaze, slow-motion orbit on level clear with confetti.

**Quality tiers (auto-selected, changeable in Settings)**
| Tier | Shadows | Bloom | MSAA | Particles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | blob shadow | off | 2× | 40% |
| Medium (Realme default) | 1024 PCF | quarter-res | 4× | 70% |
| High | 2048 PCF | half-res | 4× | 100% |
Auto-tier watches frame time and steps down if the game drops below 55 fps for 3 s. Optional 90 Hz mode for phones that support it (your Realme does).
