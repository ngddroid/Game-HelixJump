Privacy Policy

How Spindle Drop handles your information, in plain English.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

At a glance

  • Your progress, settings, coins and unlocked items stay on your device.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • This version has no internet access, no ads and no analytics.
  • We never collect, sell or share your personal data.

Who we are

Spindle Drop is a mobile arcade game made by NG Studio (“we”, “us” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses, where it is kept and what choices you have.

It applies to the current version of Spindle Drop for Android.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your game between sessions, Spindle Drop saves a small amount of information in its private storage on your device:

  • Game progress, such as unlocked levels, stars, best scores, missions and daily rewards.
  • Settings, such as sound, music, vibration, spin sensitivity and graphics quality.
  • Coins, gems and other in-game balances.
  • Unlocked and equipped items, such as balls, trails and themes.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

If you have turned on Android’s own device backup, Android may include this saved game data in your personal backup. Google handles that backup under your account settings. NG Studio cannot access it.

What we don’t collect

Spindle Drop does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. The game does not ask for, collect or use:

  • your name, email address or phone number;
  • your location, contacts, photos or files;
  • advertising IDs or other device identifiers;
  • information about other apps on your device.

There is no account and no sign-in. You can start playing straight away without telling us anything about yourself.

Internet, ads and analytics

The current version of Spindle Drop:

  • does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data over the internet;
  • does not include any advertising SDK;
  • does not include analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.

Permissions

The game uses only one Android permission:

  • Vibration: used only for haptic feedback, such as a short buzz when the ball smashes through a ring. You can turn vibration off at any time in Settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your saved data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • use Reset progress in the game’s Settings to erase your progress, coins and unlocked items; or
  • uninstall Spindle Drop to remove all of its data from your device. You can also clear the app’s storage from your device’s Android settings.

We never receive your data, so we can’t restore lost progress for you. If Android device backup is on, a copy in your personal backup may be restored if you reinstall the game. You can manage this in your device’s backup settings.

Children’s privacy

Spindle Drop is a casual game for players of all ages. It is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game doesn’t collect or share personal data, so we don’t receive any information about children who play it.

Parents and guardians can contact us using the details below with any questions.

If we add ads or analytics later

A future version might add optional features. Examples are rewarded ads, where you choose to watch an ad for an in-game reward, or analytics that help us fix bugs and improve levels.

If that happens, we will update this Privacy Policy before that version is released. The updated policy will explain:

  • what data is involved and why it is needed;
  • which third-party services receive it;
  • the choices you have, such as opting out of personalised ads where available.

Security

Your saved game data is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The game doesn’t send data anywhere, so none of it travels over a network.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the game changes. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the game or on its store listing before it takes effect.
