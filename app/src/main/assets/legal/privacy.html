Privacy Policy

How Spindle Drop handles your information, in plain English.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

At a glance

  • Your progress, settings, coins and unlocked items stay on your device.
  • No account and no sign-in — we never ask for your name, email or location.
  • We use Google Firebase to collect anonymous usage and crash data, so we can fix bugs and improve the game.
  • We send notifications: daily reminders and occasional news. You control them in your device settings.
  • There are no ads, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Spindle Drop is a mobile arcade game made by NG Studio (“we”, “us” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses, where it is kept and what choices you have.

It applies to the current version of Spindle Drop for Android.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your game between sessions, Spindle Drop saves a small amount of information in its private storage on your device:

  • Game progress, such as unlocked levels, stars, best scores, missions and daily rewards.
  • Settings, such as sound, music, vibration, spin sensitivity and graphics quality.
  • Coins, gems and other in-game balances.
  • Unlocked and equipped items, such as balls, trails and themes.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

If you have turned on Android’s own device backup, Android may include this saved game data in your personal backup. Google handles that backup under your account settings. NG Studio cannot access it.

What we don’t collect

Spindle Drop does not ask for, collect or use:

  • your name, email address or phone number;
  • your location, contacts, photos or files;
  • the device advertising ID (we switch it off for analytics);
  • information about other apps on your device.

There is no account and no sign-in, and no ads. You can start playing straight away without telling us anything about yourself. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Usage data, crash reports and messaging

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, Spindle Drop uses Google Firebase, a set of services provided by Google. Firebase works without any account or sign-in and does not identify you by name. Specifically, the game uses:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous, aggregated usage data, such as which screens are opened, which levels are started, completed or failed, time spent, your approximate region (from your IP address, which is not stored by us) and general device/app details (model, Android version, app version, language). This is tied to a random, resettable app-instance identifier, not to you.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — if the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a diagnostic report (the error, a stack trace, the app version and basic device state) so we can fix it.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — lets us send you push notifications (see “Notifications” below). Each install is given a messaging token; it does not identify you personally.

We have turned off the advertising ID and ad-personalisation signals for analytics. This data is processed by Google as our service provider under Google’s own privacy terms. See Third-party services below.

Notifications

Spindle Drop can send you two kinds of notifications:

  • Daily reminders — friendly nudges at around 9 AM and 7 PM, created on your device, to help you keep your streak going.
  • News & events — occasional push messages about new content, events and rewards, sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

On Android 13 and later the game asks for your permission before it can post notifications. You can turn notifications on or off at any time — for the whole app or per category — in your device’s Settings > Apps > Spindle Drop > Notifications. Turning them off does not affect the rest of the game.

Permissions

The game uses these Android permissions:

  • Vibration: haptic feedback, such as a short buzz when the ball smashes through a ring. You can turn vibration off in Settings.
  • Internet / network state: to send anonymous analytics and crash reports, receive push notifications and check Google Play for app updates.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the daily reminders and news notifications described above.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): so the daily reminders can be re-scheduled after your device restarts.

App updates

Spindle Drop uses Google Play in-app updates to let you install new versions without leaving the game. This feature is provided by Google Play; the update check happens through the Play Store and does not involve any personal data of yours.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your saved data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • use Reset progress in the game’s Settings to erase your progress, coins and unlocked items; or
  • uninstall Spindle Drop to remove all of its data from your device. You can also clear the app’s storage from your device’s Android settings.

Your game progress lives only on your device, so we can’t restore lost progress for you. If Android device backup is on, a copy in your personal backup may be restored if you reinstall the game. You can manage this in your device’s backup settings. To stop analytics and crash data going forward, you can turn off notifications and, if you prefer, uninstall the game; uninstalling also removes its Firebase identifiers from your device.

Children’s privacy

Spindle Drop is a casual game for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics and crash data we use is anonymous and tied to a random app identifier, not to a person, and we have disabled advertising identifiers and ad personalisation.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, or you have any questions as a parent or guardian, please contact us using the details below.

Third-party services

We use the following Google services to run and improve the game. They act as our service providers and process data under their own terms:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging) — see the Firebase Privacy and Security information and Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play (in-app updates) — governed by the Google Play Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

We don’t use advertising networks, and we don’t share your data with anyone for advertising or for their own independent use.

If we add ads later

This version has no ads. If a future version adds optional features such as rewarded ads, we will update this Privacy Policy before that version is released and explain what data is involved, who receives it and the choices you have.

Security

Your saved game progress is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The analytics, crash and messaging data described above is sent to Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we rely on Google’s infrastructure and keep the data we use to the minimum needed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the game changes. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the game or on its store listing before it takes effect.
